### Pro warns before lowering the speaker's safety margin
In stereo, Awinic's speaker protection is off for media, and the 6 dB stereo attenuation is the margin that stands in for it (CMF Stereo never goes below it). CMF Stereo Pro now asks before setting it lower, and says what is at stake:

- with the module's protection running, its limiter still holds the speaker's peaks, but Awinic's thermal protection is off in stereo, so long loud listening can overheat the speaker;
- without it (after a live switch to stereo from a mono boot, until the next reboot), only the amp's own cut-outs protect the speaker.

A red line under the field shows the same while a value under 6 is typed.

Installs straight over v1.0.95-beta: no uninstall needed.
