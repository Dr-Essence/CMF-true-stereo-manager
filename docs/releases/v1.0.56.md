### What's new
- **Stereo protection settings** for module [v1.0.4](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo/releases/latest), shown in Stereo:
  - **Speaker boost** (0–6): a louder speaker, with its loudest moments kept where the stock protection keeps them. 0 sounds as before.
  - **Deep bass guard** (Off / Light / Mid / Strong): holds back only the deepest bass, and only when it gets loud. Quiet bass is untouched.
  - **Earpiece limiter**: stops the earpiece crackling at its very loudest.
- **Pro** shows whether the protection runs, with the measured numbers behind each setting.

Update the module to v1.0.4 too: the protection lives there.

Installs straight over v1.0.53: no uninstall needed.
