### Left and right follow the screen, in every text
Both apps, the Stereo speakers page in Nothing Settings and the Sound row no longer say "earpiece left, speaker right". Left and right follow how you hold the phone (module v0.9.5+): with the earpiece on your left it plays the left channel, turned the other way it plays the right. The texts now say so.

Nothing else changes.

Installs straight over v1.0.71: no uninstall needed. Module [v1.0.8](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo/releases/latest) has the same fix in its own texts.
